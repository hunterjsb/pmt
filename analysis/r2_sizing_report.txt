  READ THE min_ask=0.00 TABLE AS A FAILURE MODE, NOT A RECOMMENDATION.
  Calibrated Kelly on a stated fair is a function of (p_cal - c)/(1-c), and
  c is the BOOK's ask. Every loss window in this corpus has the same shape:
  the book collapses on our side while the model stays pinned near 1.0. A
  collapsed ask therefore reads to Kelly as a gigantic edge, so uncapped
  calibrated Kelly puts its LARGEST bets into precisely the windows that
  lose. That is why the min_ask=0.00 cells lose more than the flat clips the
  engine actually traded, and why the per-clip sub-cap alone cannot fix it:
  the sub-cap limits one clip, but a collapsing book emits many clips.
